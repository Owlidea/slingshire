Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

In short

Slingshire does not ask you to create an account. We do not collect your name, email or phone number. Your progress and settings are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device

Level progress, stars, gold, purchased items, settings (sound, music, haptics, language) and daily reward information are stored on your device. They are removed when you uninstall the app and are not transferred to another device automatically.

Ads

The app shows Google AdMob ads. AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), your IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads. You can change your personalised-ads choice in your device ad settings or in the consent screen the app shows. The "Remove Ads" purchase removes forced interstitial ads; optional rewarded videos remain.

Purchases

Purchases are made through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. We use RevenueCat to manage purchases; it processes an anonymous user ID and purchase records to verify your purchase status.

Notifications

If you choose, daily reward reminders are shown. They are scheduled on the device, not sent from a server. You can turn them off any time in your device settings.

Children

Slingshire is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

Third parties

Google (AdMob) and RevenueCat operate under their own privacy policies. We do not sell or share your data for other purposes.

Your rights

Under applicable laws (GDPR, KVKK) you may access, correct and delete your data. Because we hold no data that identifies you, most data is only on your device; for questions or requests, write to us: support@owlidea.com

Changes

We may update this policy. Important changes will be published on this page.
