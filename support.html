My Remove Ads purchase is missing

Tap "Restore Purchases" in the Settings screen. Make sure you are signed in with the App Store or Google Play account you purchased with.

I bought gold but it was not added

Close and reopen the app. If the problem continues, write to us with your purchase receipt.

Can I move my progress to another device?

No. Game progress is stored only on your device. Remove Ads can be restored on your other devices.

I would like a refund

Refunds are handled by the App Store or Google Play under their own policies.
